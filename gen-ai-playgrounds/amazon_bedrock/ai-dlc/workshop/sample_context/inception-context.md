This document contains background information that helps ground the work we do together.
It reflects an understanding of the user, the problem as they experience it, what has
worked poorly in the past, and what success should feel like.

---

## The User

- An individual managing their own work
- Cares about speed, clarity, and minimal effort
- Wants to capture work items quickly
- Wants to review what they’ve captured without friction
- Loses interest when tools feel complex or distracting

---

## The Problem (In the User’s Words)

- “I just want a simple way to track what I’m working on.”
- “I don’t want another system I have to learn.”
- “If it takes too long, I won’t use it.”
- “I want to see what I’ve already noted.”

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Capturing a work item is immediate
- Reviewing work items is immediate
- Behavior is obvious without instructions
- Nothing distracts from the core interaction

---

## What Has Been Tried (and Didn’t Work)

- Feature-heavy tools felt overwhelming
- Systems requiring setup or configuration were abandoned
- Anything delaying the first useful interaction failed

---

## Constraints That Matter

- Scope must remain small
- Focus on clarity over completeness
- Avoid functionality unrelated to the core problem

---

## How We’ll Know It Worked

- A user can use it without explanation
- The behavior matches how the user describes the problem
- The experience feels simple rather than impressive
